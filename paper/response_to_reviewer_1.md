# Response to Reviewer 1 (standalone; not shown to other reviewers)

We thank the reviewer for a detailed and careful reading of the mathematics. All concerns have been addressed in the revised manuscript; below each comment is quoted in paraphrase, followed by the action taken and its location. Revised passages are quoted in italics.

**M1 (Major, blocking). Lemma 3.2's proof contains a false intermediate inequality and does not derive the stated constant.**
*Fixed.* The proof now uses the chi-square route: KL <= chi^2(P||Q) = (p-q)^2/(q(1-q)) <= h^2/(16 p0(1-p0)) = C_B h^2, with C_B redefined as 1/(16 sigma(-2B)(1-sigma(-2B))). The false reverse-Pinsker step is removed. The new constant is propagated through both parts of Theorem 4.1, whose statement now reads (a) T >= log(1/(4 delta))/(4 C_B Delta^2) and (b) T >= m log(1/(4 delta))/(alpha C_B n lambda_G Delta^2). Location: Lemma 3.2 and Theorem 4.1, Section 4.

**M2 (Major). Theorem 4.1(b)'s extremal-entry claim (u_max >= 1/sqrt(n)) is false.**
*Fixed.* Replaced by the Popoviciu variance-range argument: a mean-zero unit vector has variance 1/n, so (max u - min u)^2 >= 4/n, which is what the proof needs. The final constant of part (b) is now m/(alpha C_B n lambda_G Delta^2), matching what the corrected chain delivers (this also incorporates the constant observation made under the proof-consistency comment below). Location: proof of Theorem 4.1(b), Section 4.

**M3 (Major). Proposition 5.2's sketch has a screen inconsistent with Theorem 5.1's kappa-dependent bound, and the budget does not follow.**
*Fixed.* Proposition 5.2 is restated with a data-independent screen tau_ij = 2 rho_r, rho_r = C kappa_0 sqrt(log(8 n^2 r/delta)/(sigma_min c_r lambda_G)), carrying the entrywise constants, and is now proved in full (conditional on the entrywise guarantee) rather than sketched: a union bound over rounds gives the good event, members are never eliminated by induction, and every non-member is eliminated once c_r >= m-tilde, with budget 2 m_edges m-tilde where m-tilde equals the one-shot m* up to log factors. A new remark explicitly separates this screen from the calibrated two-term radius used by Algorithm 1 (the inconsistency the reviewer identified). Location: Proposition 5.2, its proof, and Remark 5.3, Section 5.

**M4 (Major, blocking). Theorem 5.1 delegates its substantive step to cited results whose hypotheses are neither restated nor verified, and "regularized" is ambiguous.**
*Fixed.* The entrywise guarantee is now stated verbatim inside Theorem 5.1 as an explicit assumption (norm-uniform error C kappa_0 sqrt(log(n/delta)/(sigma_min m lambda_G)) with probability 1-delta for the constrained MLE on any connected weighted graph with per-edge counts at least m), with an honest statement that the regularity conditions of the cited analyses have not been verified for arbitrary deterministic graphs, so the theorem is conditional on this guarantee. The Fisher-information computation is retained as motivation for the lambda_G dependence. The estimator is renamed throughout: "gauge-constrained maximum-likelihood estimate" (the sum-to-zero gauge), no longer "regularized". Location: Theorem 5.1 and proof, Section 5; abstract; Section 6.

**M5 (Major). Remark 4.3's path illustration is quantitatively wrong.**
*Fixed.* The remark is rewritten in terms of the concentration ratio m max_e (u_i-u_j)^2/lambda_G: graphs with O(1) ratio (including paths, whose Fiedler energy is nearly uniform) are covered by Theorem 4.1(b) for all algorithms, while graphs with concentrated Fiedler energy (two cliques joined by a bridge) are where adaptivity can help. The E2 discussion now cites the remark in this corrected form. Location: Remark 4.3, Section 4; Section 6.3.
